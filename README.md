# Café Calle Ocho

## EGN 475 Flask Prototyping Project

### Project Choice

**Option A: Café Calle Ocho - Little Havana**

I chose Café Calle Ocho because a simple ordering system can help customers order Cuban food and drinks without waiting in long lines. It can also help kitchen staff organize incoming orders and track their progress.

### Problem Statement

Café Calle Ocho currently handles customer orders through direct communication with café staff, which can make it difficult to organize orders and track their progress. This causes delays, confusion, and unnecessary waiting for customers and kitchen staff. Our prototype will provide an online menu and ordering system where customers can select items, submit pickup orders, and view order statuses. Kitchen staff will be able to manage orders through received, preparing, ready, and picked up statuses so that the café can organize orders more efficiently and provide a better customer experience.

### Target Users

#### Persona 1: Carlos Martinez

**Role:** Local resident and regular café customer.

**Main Goals:**

* Browse Cuban food and drinks.
* Check prices before ordering.
* Place pickup orders quickly.
* Know when an order is ready.

**Frustrations:**

* Waiting in long lines.
* Asking staff about menu items and prices.
* Not knowing when an order will be ready.

**Technology Comfort:** High. Uses a smartphone regularly.

**Quote:** "I want to order my coffee and sandwich before I arrive so I don't have to wait in line."

#### Persona 2: Maria Rodriguez

**Role:** Kitchen staff member.

**Main Goals:**

* See incoming orders in one place.
* Know which orders need preparation.
* Update order statuses quickly.
* Avoid missing or mixing up orders.

**Frustrations:**

* Managing several orders during busy hours.
* Confusion about which order came first.
* Difficulty tracking pending and completed orders.

**Technology Comfort:** Moderate to high. Comfortable using a computer or tablet.

**Quote:** "I need one clear screen that shows what to prepare next and which orders are ready."

### Must-Have Features

1. Display a menu with categories, item names, prices, and descriptions.
2. Allow customers to select items and quantities.
3. Allow customers to review their order and total price.
4. Collect the customer's name and phone number.
5. Submit pickup orders without scheduled pickup times.
6. Display incoming orders on a kitchen management board.
7. Allow kitchen staff to update order statuses and view order history.

### Out of Scope

The following features are not included in this prototype:

1. Online payment or credit card processing.
2. Home delivery services.
3. SMS or email order notifications.
4. Customer accounts and password registration.
5. Loyalty points, rewards, or discount programs.
